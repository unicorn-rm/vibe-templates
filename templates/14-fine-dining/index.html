I · The idea

We cook the way our grandparents lit a room: one flame, held close, so you notice everything it touches.

Ombre is a twenty-seat dining room with no gas line. Every plate passes through the hearth, the smoke cupboard or the ember bed before it reaches you.

The menu changes with what the forest, the lake and two small farms give us that week. We write it on Tuesday and burn the draft on Sunday.

    III · The hand

    Ilya Varga

    Trained in Copenhagen and Lyon, Ilya came home to cook with the things he grew up foraging. He believes a menu should feel like a walk, not a lecture.

    Before Ombre he spent four years cooking on open fire in a mountain lodge with no electricity after nine in the evening. It shows.
